Designed so even Entrix
can’t open your PC.

Every approval is signed by your iPhone, and the server never holds what it would need to sign in as you.

What Entrix knows

  • That a sign-in was requested, on which of your PCs, and your answer.
  • The names you give your computers.
  • Security alerts your PCs send, like a wrong password.
  • Your Sign in with Apple account, and whether you have Premium.

What Entrix never gets

  • Your Windows password.
  • Your Entrix PIN.
  • Your recovery code or 2-step secret in readable form.
  • The key your iPhone signs approvals with.
  • Your face. Face ID never leaves your iPhone.
  • Your card. Apple handles every payment.

Signed by your iPhone.

Each approval and each remote lock carries a signature only your iPhone can make. Not even the Entrix server can fake one.

End-to-end encrypted.

Your 2-step secret and recovery code travel between PC and iPhone encrypted with a key the server doesn’t have.

Face ID or Touch ID, every time.

An approval needs your face or fingerprint. A passcode alone isn’t enough.

Works when the internet doesn’t.

If the PC can’t reach Entrix, your 6-digit code and recovery code still let you in.

Deny means locked.

Questions.

When can I get Entrix?

Soon. Entrix is in final testing on iPhone and Windows. Tap “Notify me at launch” or write to entrixsupport@gmail.com, and we’ll tell you the day it’s out.

Who makes Entrix?

An independent developer, not a big company. Questions and feedback go straight to the developer at entrixsupport@gmail.com.

What happens if I lose my iPhone?

Your recovery code signs you in at the PC without the phone, so keep a copy of it somewhere safe, away from your iPhone.

What if my PC is offline?

Type the 6-digit 2-step code from the Entrix app, or from any authenticator app you added it to. It doesn’t need the internet.

Do I need Premium?

No. Free protects one computer, with Face ID approvals, the 2-step code, the recovery code and security alerts. Premium adds Lock Screen approvals, more computers and remote lock, restart and shut down.

Can Entrix see my Windows password?

No. Your password never reaches the Entrix server. Neither does your Entrix PIN or your recovery code.

Which Windows accounts does it work with?

Local accounts and Microsoft accounts, on Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. The Entrix app runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later.
